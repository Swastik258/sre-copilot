# Clear-water design system

Tarn is named for a still mountain lake: the surface is what the system
observed, depth is the evidence behind it, and the reflection is a diagnosis
that an operator can inspect. The interface should feel quiet and precise,
not like a generic dark SaaS dashboard.

## Theme and tokens

All palette, type, layout, and motion tokens live in
[`frontend/app/theme.css`](../frontend/app/theme.css). Shared component and
layout rules live in `frontend/app/globals.css`.

| Mode | `data-theme` | Surface | Text |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Editorial | `paper` | Bone paper `#EFEBE2` | Ink `#10201F` |
| Operations | `ink` | Ink water `#0B1416`, raised surface `#101C1F` | Bone `#EFEBE2` |

The shared accent is glacial `#8FD3D9`. Amber `#E8A94B` is reserved for
hypotheses and warnings; brick `#D9604C` is reserved for errors. Hairlines on
the ink surface use `rgba(190,225,230,0.12)`. Primary actions invert the
current foreground and background.

Marketing, documentation, resources, and authentication use `paper`.
Operational pages and the dashboard use `ink`. Components should use semantic
CSS variables (`--bg`, `--text`, `--border`, and related roles) rather than
branching on the active theme.

## Type

- **Newsreader** is the display serif for headlines and editorial statements.
- **Inter Tight** is the UI grotesk for body text, navigation, and controls.
- **JetBrains Mono** is reserved for evidence references, code, and timestamps.

Decorative labels are ordinary UI text, not uppercase mono eyebrows.

## Visual language

- Contour lines appear as a restrained background texture and as the Tarn
  wordmark in `frontend/public/tarn-wordmark.svg` and
  `frontend/public/tarn-wordmark-ink.svg`.
- Diagnosis output uses a left-margin depth scale for observations, evidence,
  and hypotheses. Evidence IDs are inline references, not status pills.
- Whitespace, typographic scale, and hairlines establish hierarchy. Avoid
  numbered feature cards, nested frames, gradients, glows, and decorative
  status badges.
- Editorial pages alternate bone and ink sections. The dashboard remains an
  ink surface with plain tables and rules rather than a grid of cards.

## Demo and product truth

The no-signup demo presents snapshots generated by the existing deterministic
Kubernetes diagnosis rules against the checked-in fixture. It does not call a
backend endpoint, require an account, or connect to a cluster. Refresh these
snapshots when the fixture or diagnosis rules change; do not replace them with
hand-authored success-shaped examples.

Feature states are factual: `Live` means the route and behavior exist in this
checkout; paid tiers remain `Planned` until billing and purchase flows ship.
Customer names, testimonials, and outcome metrics must not be invented.

## Motion and accessibility

Scroll reveals are subtle, run once, and must not affect document flow. Contour
drift is slow and is disabled for `prefers-reduced-motion: reduce`. Keep
keyboard focus visible, retain semantic headings and labels, and use the
shared skip link and page landmarks.
